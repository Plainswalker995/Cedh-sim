Combo puzzles for the trust scorecard: each file gives your deck the pieces of a winning line on its first turn.
Lines start with "hero" (your deck) or p0..p3 (seats in turn order), then hand / battlefield / graveyard / top,
then card names separated by semicolons. A puzzle counts as converted when your deck wins by combo on its first turn.
Opponents may still interact before or during your turn, as at a real table.
